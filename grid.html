Grid Challenge

Power of Attention, Memory

Firstly you will be shown a number of circles scrambled at different positions and one of the circles blinks. You have to remember the position of the circle which blinks. Then you will be shown two patterns with grids arranged randomly. You have to find whether the given two patterns are symmetric or not. This will repeat for three times. In these three turns you must remember the three different positions of circles and also answer whether the given two patterns are symmetric or not. Then you will be again shown the number of circles scrambled at different positions. Now you have to mark the three circles which you were asked to remember in the same order you were shown.

How to Play

  • Correct answer will add the score by 3 points.
  • Wrong answer will subtract the score by 1 point.
  • Highlighted circle appears only 2 sec.
  • The two patterns appear for 6 seconds.
